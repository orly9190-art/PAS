Sustainability

Quality, safety, and the environment on every voyage

What we uphold so operations run without harming our crew, the waters, or the communities around them.

Policy

We operate our sea transport business by complying with strict safety and risk procedures, raising the competency of our people, strengthening technical capability, and proactively managing docking time so the fleet stays dependable for our customers.

Three Pillars

How we put it into practice

Environment

Pollution prevention is upheld through SNPP and IOPP certificates, anti fouling coating, and waste logging in the Garbage Record Book on every vessel.

Social

Crew safety comes first: task-appropriate protective equipment, regular emergency drills, plus health books and sanitation certificates on every vessel.

Governance

The Board of Commissioners oversees and the Board of Directors executes, supported by a code of conduct, risk management, internal audit, and a whistleblowing channel that protects reporter confidentiality.

Daily Practice

What happens every day

Scheduled maintenance

Maintenance and docking are planned rather than reactive.

Crew competency

Seafarer certificates matching each rank, with regular training.

Emergency readiness

Safety equipment is inspected and radio documents kept valid.

Incident reporting

Every incident and near miss is logged and followed up.
