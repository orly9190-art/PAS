Our Services

Marine transport with our own fleet

Four forms of engagement, backed by 80 tug and barge sets we operate and manage.

01

Transshipment

Carriage of commodities from mine jetties to a quay or mother bulk vessel at the transshipment point, using tug and barge convoys.

Request a Quote
02

Inter-Island Shipping

Carriage of commodities from the origin port to a domestic inter-island destination port, on a schedule agreed in the contract.

Request a Quote
03

Fleet Chartering

Tug and barge sets on time charter or voyage charter, with certified crew and routine maintenance included.

Request a Quote
04

Partner Vessel Re-Charter

At peak times we supply vessels from partner owners, managed to the same operating standard.
