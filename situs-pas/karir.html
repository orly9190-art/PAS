Career Paths

Two career tracks

Vessel Crew

Working aboard tug boats on rotation, with seafarer certificates matching the rank.

  • Master and Deck Officers
  • Chief Engineer and Engineers
  • Helmsman and Oiler
  • Deckhand and Cook

Shore Team

Supporting fleet operations from the Jakarta head office and the Samarinda and Banjarmasin site offices.

  • Operations and Port Captain
  • Engineering and Vessel Maintenance
  • Commercial and Chartering
  • Finance, HR, and Internal Audit
