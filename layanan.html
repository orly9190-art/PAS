Marine Logistics Services

PT Prima Armada Samudra provides marine transport services across the waters of South and East Kalimantan

With the tug and barge fleet we operate and manage, we carry bulk cargo from loading points along the Barito and Mahakam rivers to transshipment points and destination ports.

Most mining sites in Kalimantan have no direct link to a deep-water port that large vessels can berth at. That is where we come in. Our tug and barge convoys bridge the gap, moving commodities from river jetties to mother vessels offshore, so large volumes can reach domestic and export markets efficiently and economically.

Sailing area: the waters of South Kalimantan (Barito River, Taboneo) and East Kalimantan (Mahakam River, Muara Berau, Muara Jawa).
