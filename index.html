About Us

A bulk cargo transport partner in Indonesian waters

PT Prima Armada Samudra is an Indonesian shipping company engaged in domestic sea transport using tug boats and barges. It was founded on 30 March 2004 and is headquartered in Central Jakarta.

We currently own, operate, and manage 80 sets of tug boats and barges. Every vessel flies the Indonesian flag and is classed with RINA or ClassNK, run by crew certified for their rank.

We take on the marine logistics side of our clients’ business so they can focus on their core activity. We keep communication open so the partnership stays mutually beneficial over the long term.

We believe our people are our main asset. Crew training runs regularly and we maintain a safe working environment, so our marine standards stay current.
